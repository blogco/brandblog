Managing Over 2,000 Solar Sites in Singapore

June 05, 2026 BrandBlog Editorial

Rooftop solar is easy to imagine and hard to run at scale. Two thousand sites scattered across industrial estates, reservoirs and buildings is not a single power plant — it is a distributed network that has to be monitored, maintained and optimised every single day.

Operations at a distance

When sites are spread across a country, you cannot rely on people driving between them. The answer is data: remote monitoring flags underperformance, and analytics prioritise which site needs attention first.

It is still a people business

  • Technicians who can diagnose and repair across many different setups.
  • Engineers who tune performance and manage grid interactions.
  • Partners who own the roofs and share the benefits.

Technology tells you where to look. People are what fix it.

The bigger picture

Distributed solar turns thousands of buildings into contributors to the grid. Managing that fleet well is what turns an ambitious target into dependable, everyday clean power.
