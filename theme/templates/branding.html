03 — Typography

Typeface

The brand typeface is Frutiger, a humanist sans-serif. Because Frutiger is licensed and cannot be redistributed on the web, this build uses a faithful fallback stack:

Frutiger, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif
