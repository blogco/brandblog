Summary: A resilient first half anchored by growth in renewables and integrated urban solutions, with disciplined capital allocation across the group.

Singapore, 13 August 2026 — The first half of 2026 marked another step in the group's
transition from a traditional energy player to a broad-based provider of energy and urban
solutions. Performance was anchored by a larger renewables portfolio, a steadier
contribution from gas and related services, and disciplined capital allocation.

## Highlights of the period

- Renewable energy capacity continued to grow across wind, solar and energy storage.
- Integrated urban solutions delivered resilient, recurring income.
- The group maintained a strong balance sheet to fund future growth.

> Our purpose remains unchanged: to do good and play our part in building a sustainable
> future — while creating lasting value for shareholders and communities.

## Growing the transition portfolio

Management reiterated that the energy transition is a multi-decade journey. The group is
balancing returns today with investment in the low-carbon infrastructure that will define
the next decade: renewable imports, energy storage, and decarbonisation solutions for
industry.

## Looking ahead

The second half will focus on executing the committed project pipeline, integrating recent
acquisitions, and sharpening operational efficiency. The group expects the transition
portfolio to contribute a larger share of earnings as new capacity comes online.
