Summary: Treating water as a resource rather than a waste stream closes the loop between industry, cities and the environment.

Water is the quiet enabler of urban life. Behind every tap is an energy-intensive,
engineering-heavy system that most people never see — and one that is ripe for reinvention.

## From disposal to recovery

Modern treatment is less about getting rid of water and more about recovering what is
inside it: clean water for reuse, nutrients for agriculture, and biogas for energy.

- **Membrane and biological treatment** deliver high-quality recycled water for industry.
- **Energy recovery** turns a treatment plant from a consumer into a producer.
- **Digital monitoring** catches problems early and cuts chemical and energy use.

## Closing the loop

When wastewater becomes a resource, industrial estates and cities can operate with far less
freshwater withdrawal — a critical advantage in water-scarce regions.
