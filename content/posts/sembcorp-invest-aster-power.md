structured to align incentives over the long term.

## What it signals

- Continued conviction in integrated energy solutions.
- A preference for partnerships that de-risk execution.
- A focus on markets with strong demand and clear policy support.

As the energy transition accelerates, collaboration — not solo execution — is becoming the
decisive advantage.
