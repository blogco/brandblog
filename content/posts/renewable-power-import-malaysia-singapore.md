Summary: Conditional approval paves the way for cross-border renewable electricity imports, strengthening regional grid resilience and decarbonisation.

Singapore, 7 August 2026 — Conditional approval has been granted for a renewable power
import project that would bring clean electricity from Peninsular Malaysia into Singapore,
advancing the region's ambition to decarbonise its power mix.

## Why cross-border imports matter

Land-constrained cities like Singapore cannot scale renewables alone. Regional
interconnection allows clean power generated where land and resources are abundant to flow
to where demand is concentrated — a cornerstone of a credible net-zero pathway.

## What the project involves

- Development of renewable generation capacity across the border.
- Transmission infrastructure to deliver power reliably into Singapore.
- Progressive commissioning, with capacity ramping up in phases.

The approval is a milestone, not the finish line. Final investment decisions will depend on
commercial agreements, grid readiness and regulatory conditions.
